# Product Overview — Youjian （游鉴）

## The problem

Game accounts and items are valuable, illiquid, and traded in scattered chat groups where fraud is the default risk. Sellers can't reach buyers efficiently; buyers can't verify what they're paying for; prices are folklore.

## The product

A single marketplace that organizes the whole trade into halls, each shaped to how that segment actually transacts:

- **Marketplace** for straight listings
- **Auctions** when an account's price is genuinely unknown
- **Buy orders** so demand can be posted, not just supply
- **Rentals** for players who want access without ownership
- **Boosting & companions** for services around the game, not just assets in it
- **Items** for the long tail of equipment and materials

## Operating model

- Founder-operated: listings review, dispute handling and hall rules are run by the person who built it
- Mobile-first growth: the desktop platform proved the halls; the mobile app (v1, five tabs, full publish/order/bargain loop) is where new users arrive
- Trust as the product: drafts, order records and in-app bargaining keep negotiation inside the platform instead of disappearing into private chats

## Roadmap

- **Nov 2026:** full public launch with mobile v1
- **Post-launch:** task hall opening, richer seller tooling, category expansion driven by hall-level demand data
